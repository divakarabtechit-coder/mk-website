About MK Reinforcement Sections · Est. 1995 · Keeranatham, Coimbatore

Empowering uPVC Window and Door Manufacturers Since 1995

What began in 1995 as a modest fabrication workshop led by founder S. Manikandan with one shearing machine and one welding unit has evolved into one of South India's most dependable cold roll-forming steel reinforcement partners. Today, from our manufacturing plant in Keeranatham near Saravanampatti, Coimbatore, our 15-member specialist team produces over 150 tonnes of precision galvanized sections every month.

To empower uPVC window and door manufacturers across South India with high-precision, zero-defect cold roll-formed steel reinforcement sections that maximize fabrication line speed, eliminate cutting wastage, and guarantee decades of structural rigidity.

Our Vision

To be South India's most trusted and technologically advanced fenestration reinforcement partner—recognized as the benchmark in millimeter-level dimensional accuracy, coastal-grade zinc protection, and fabricator-first supply chain dependability.
