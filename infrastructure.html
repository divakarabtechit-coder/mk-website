Advanced Roll-Forming Machinery

5+ automated continuous high-speed cold roll-forming lines guaranteeing crisp geometric lines, cold-worked tensile stability, and ±0.20 mm millimeter-level tolerances across uPVC steel reinforcements and GI channels.

Slitting & Synchronized Cutting Units

Heavy-duty rotary coil slitters and synchronized flying shears supplying both standardized stock lengths and custom fabricator-cut lengths to eliminate assembly waste.

±0.15 mm Strip Width Precision

Press Tool & Sheet Metal Infrastructure

Mechanical presses and hardened custom tool-and-die blocks engineered for continuous inline punching, drainage slots, lock cutouts, and multi-axis bending.

Inline Hardware Anchoring

Weather-Sheltered Coil Storage & Logistics

Dedicated indoor moisture-sheltered coil bays safeguard hot-dip galvanized master coils prior to slitting, preserving uniform zinc passivation integrity before every production run.

Overhead Crane Coil Handling · 1.0 mm to 3.0 mm Stock Gauges

Integrated Quality Control (QC) Lab

Every finished section batch passes through calibrated micrometer wall-thickness checks, Go/No-Go uPVC chamber fitment verification, zinc uniformity inspection, and longitudinal torsion straightness testing prior to South India dispatch.
